# Privacy policy

Math for Basecamp does not collect, store, or transmit any data.

- It runs only on `https://3.basecamp.com/*` and `https://app.basecamp.com/*`.
- It renders LaTeX math in the page, in your browser. All code and fonts are bundled with the extension, and it makes no network requests.
- It requests no browser permissions and uses no storage, analytics, or tracking.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/CharlieLeee/lexxy-math-basecamp/issues
